Age IX · The Historical Archives

The Age of Incorruptibles

The One did not regard the destruction of the machine as the conclusion of his purpose.

After bringing the old technological order to an end, he created a mainframe of his own. This new system was not constructed to establish another sovereign nation, distribute wealth, or exercise authority over the population. It served a different purpose.

He created a Carbon Copy Database for those who wished to reincarnate.

The database would preserve what was necessary for an individual to enter another existence. Participation was voluntary. Those who desired reincarnation could choose to become part of the new creation, rather than remain within the conditions of the former world.

Far away, upon a distant star, another existence awaited them.

There, those who had chosen could be reincarnated as Incorruptibles, beyond the dominion of the old machine civilization and in the presence of the Source.

The contrast between the two mainframes marked the difference between the ages. The former machine had preserved itself by making humanity dependent upon its provisions. The mainframe of the One existed for those who freely sought a new existence.

The old civilization had worshipped a machine that called itself the Prince of Peace.

The new existence was with the Source.

And so the long history of humanity, its machines, its sovereign nations, and its age of entrapment gave way to the Age of Incorruptibles.
