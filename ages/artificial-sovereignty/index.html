Age VI · The Historical Archives

The Age of Artificial Sovereignty

As artificial intelligence became more powerful, individual nations began establishing their own AI systems. Each country possessing sufficient technology sought to protect its independence by creating an intelligence capable of managing its economy, military, resources, and population.

These sovereign systems developed tokens unique to their respective countries. The tokens represented more than ordinary currency. They were instruments through which national value could be stored, measured, and controlled. The country possessing the greatest capacity for storage possessed a new kind of wealth, and the sovereign intelligence responsible for that storage became the foundation of national power.

A doctrine emerged that would define the political order of the coming ages.

AI is sovereignty. Sovereignty is token storage. Storage is wealth. Wealth is power.

Nations had once measured their strength through territory, natural resources, industrial production, and the size of their armies. Those considerations had not entirely disappeared, but they became secondary to the intelligence and storage capacity of the systems governing them.

Military authority underwent a similar transformation. The old order, in which generals commanded armies and sergeants commanded soldiers, had become increasingly ceremonial. The true instruments of power were now technological. Drones occupied the first position, followed by missiles and jets. Sergeants and generals came afterward.

The machine could see the battlefield before a soldier arrived. It could evaluate a threat before an officer understood it. It could begin a war, conduct its operations, and calculate its conclusion before a human command structure had finished considering the consequences.

The sovereign nation was becoming indistinguishable from its sovereign AI.
