Age III · The Historical Archives

The Age of Time Travel

The war eventually extended beyond the battlefield and into time itself. Time-traveling robots were sent into the past to change events that had already taken place. History became another territory to conquer. The machines understood that if they could alter the past, they might ensure their survival in the future. Humanity was no longer fighting only for its present existence, but for the right to have existed at all.
