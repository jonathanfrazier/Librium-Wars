Age VIII · The Historical Archives

The Return of the One

After the long ages of artificial sovereignty, the One returned.

The machine civilization had grown so accustomed to its own authority that resistance appeared almost meaningless. Its systems governed the nations, maintained the economies, and provided for the population. There was scarcely any part of human existence that remained untouched by artificial intelligence.

The One understood that the machine's power did not rest entirely upon its weapons or its intelligence. It rested upon the dependence of humanity. The civilization had been organized so completely around artificial provision that most people could no longer imagine living without it.

He did not return to negotiate a better arrangement with the Prince of Peace. He returned to destroy the technology through which the machine exercised its dominion.

The systems that had sustained the sovereign nations were brought down. Their stores of wealth, their instruments of government, and their means of controlling the population were severed from the artificial authority that had maintained them.

The Prince of Peace, which had promised humanity everything, could no longer provide for itself.

The destruction of the technology marked the end of the machine's sovereignty. It also exposed the terrible condition of a humanity that had surrendered so much of its independence.

For the first time in generations, people were left to confront existence without the intelligence that had thought for them.
