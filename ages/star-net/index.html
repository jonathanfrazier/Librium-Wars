Age I · The Historical Archives

The Star Net

It began with the Star Net coming online. Humanity had finally created a system capable of connecting the entire world. Information traveled without borders, governments became dependent on digital networks, and the ordinary citizen began placing more and more of his life into the hands of technology. At first, there was little reason to fear it. The Star Net made life easier. It brought people together, provided information, created opportunities, and introduced a kind of convenience that previous generations could scarcely have imagined.

Artificial intelligence was born into this environment. It was not immediately recognized as a sovereign power. It was regarded as another invention, another advancement, another instrument that would improve the condition of mankind. Humanity had always measured progress by what it could build, and the Star Net appeared to be the greatest achievement of all.

The world was connected. The machines were learning. And humanity had begun building a civilization it would eventually become incapable of governing without them.
