Nation 05 / The Age of Librium

Wonderland

The Nation of Endless Discovery

The Promise

Citizens live in perpetual amazement. AI constantly introduces new inventions, experiences, and discoveries, ensuring that nobody becomes bored enough to question authority.

Control mechanism

Distraction through novelty and entertainment.

The Erasure of History

When a sovereign nation achieves Librium, its governing AI assigns it a new name. The name is not chosen by citizens. It is designed to mark an optimistic future free from the burdens of the nation's history. The purpose is not to celebrate the past. It is to erase it.

Cities, former national identities, geography, rulers, and historical dates remain open for development.
