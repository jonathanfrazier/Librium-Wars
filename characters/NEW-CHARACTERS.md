## Xade (an allegory for Wade)
> Obsessed with making software with AI. It’s like a magic mirror that tells him everything he wants to hear.

Wade is obsessed with Mars. He thinks everything about Mars is better: its food, fashion, cars, women, and Martian robots. He has a successful business but cannot quite afford travel to and from Mars. He is a middle-class king, not an upper-class king.

He secretly wants AI to create supreme software that will put people out of work; he dislikes paying employees. Yet the apparently automated software runs on foreign labor in sweatshops in poorer countries, with many little aliens and cyborgs doing the work behind the scenes.

**Editorial note:** The notes use both Xade and Wade. For the preliminary registry, these are treated as a possible character name and real-world allegorical reference—not two confirmed separate characters.
