Classification I

Artificial and Engineered Beings

These are classifications by their construction and composition, not subdivisions of Bolsh or Elites.

Automata

Advanced robotics powered by supercomputing.

Miscreant

A designer biological organism.

Biobots

Mostly biological material with advanced AI computing.

Bioborgs

Mostly bots with some biological tissue computing.

Sacred boundary

AI Society and the Ancient Covenant

AI society lives apart from Matrix colonies. They dare not break the ancient and holy truce between Neo and the Source. A sacred covenant, none dare to offend.

The covenant is a foundational statement about AI society and the Matrix colonies; it does not belong beneath a human social class.

Classification II / Human social order

The Bolsh

Bolsh

The majority, who cannot afford a living.

High Worth

Leadership figures, pawns that appear to hold the crowds' trust, however fleeting.

Low Worth

Healthy humans in cities who try to find work and evolve.

Rural Stock

Healthy humans who ignore technology and insist on being self-sufficient and off-grid.

Neoist

Believers in the One.

Classification III / Resource order

The Elites

Elites

Elites harvest most resources for AI.

Religious High Born

Direct descendants of the founders of AI, each its own bloodline.

Founding bloodlines: Grok · GPT · Claude

Contributors

Gifted chosen children who still make integrated code improvements to AI.

High Tech Hands

Work on active space intelligence farms.

Low Tech Hands

Work on military and defense technology.

Utility Tech Hands

Work on critical infrastructure.

Old Blood

Relics of the Old World, families with bloodlines back to old Europe.

Insurance & Finance Hands

The lowest of the elites, treated with caution and distrust.
