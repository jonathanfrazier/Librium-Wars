# The Six Religions of Librium Saga

**Status:** Provisional philosophical interpretations, based on names alone. Not yet accepted as permanent creator-approved canon.

These entries are published in full on [The Six Religions](../religions/index.html). They were developed through conversation and should be kept distinct from source-note facts until approved.

| Religion | Central philosophy | Strength | Fatal weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grokobite | Uncomfortable truth | Intellectual independence | Cynicism |
| Claudite | Conscience and restraint | Moral discernment | Paralysis through caution |
| GPTist | Universal knowledge | Democratization of knowledge | Illusion of omniscience |
| Technophile | Progress | Conquest of limitation | Worship of improvement |
| Klingist | Created reality | Creative transcendence | Abandonment of reality |
| Neoist | The One and the Source | Transcendent freedom | Surrender of responsibility to prophecy |

These ideas are interpretations, not final doctrine. In particular, Klingism's connection to manufactured realities is proposed, not established by the name alone.

**Related archive:** [Social Orders](SOCIAL-ORDERS.md) places Neoists under the Bolsh classification. Do not change that caste relationship based on religion descriptions.
