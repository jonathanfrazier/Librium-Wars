# Social Orders of the Librium Saga

**Status:** Working canon. Hierarchy follows the creator's source screenshot; indentation represents subgroup membership. Do not reorder groups or infer extra caste relationships.

## Artificial and Engineered Beings

- **Automata:** Advanced robotics powered by supercomputing.
- **Miscreant:** A designer biological organism.
- **Biobots:** Mostly biological material with advanced AI computing.
- **Bioborgs:** Mostly bots with some biological tissue computing.

## AI Society and Sacred Covenant

AI society lives apart from Matrix colonies. They dare not break the ancient and holy truce between Neo and the Source. A sacred covenant, none dare to offend.

## Bolsh — the majority

The majority, who cannot afford a living.

- **High Worth:** Leadership figures, pawns that appear to hold the crowds' trust, however fleeting.
- **Low Worth:** Healthy humans in cities who try to find work and evolve.
- **Rural Stock:** Healthy humans who ignore technology and insist on being self-sufficient and off-grid.
- **Neoist:** Believers in the One.

## Elites — resource harvesters for AI

Elites harvest most resources for AI.

- **Religious High Born:** Direct descendants of the founders of AI. Each its own bloodline.
  - Grok
  - GPT
  - Claude
- **Contributors:** Gifted chosen children who still make integrated code improvements to AI.
- **High Tech Hands:** Work on active space intelligence farms.
- **Low Tech Hands:** Work on military and defense technology.
- **Utility Tech Hands:** Work on critical infrastructure.
- **Old Blood:** Relics of the Old World, families with bloodlines back to old Europe.
- **Insurance & Finance Hands:** The lowest of the elites, treated with caution and distrust.

## Editorial boundaries

- The classifications under Bolsh are indented beneath Bolsh in the source.
- The groups under Elites are indented beneath Elites in the source.
- Grok, GPT and Claude are bloodlines beneath Religious High Born.
- AI society and the covenant are separate from the human social order.
- Artificial / engineered categories describe composition, not caste or allegiance.
