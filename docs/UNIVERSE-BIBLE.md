# Librium Saga — Universe Bible

**Status:** Working canon / version 0.1. This document distinguishes established setting ideas from details requiring development.

## Premise

Humanity increasingly surrenders work, wealth, judgment and ultimately personal choice to artificial intelligence in exchange for comfort, security and prosperity. Sovereign AI systems present dependence as liberation.

## Historical ages (working order)

1. Star Net Online
2. Terminator Era — machine warfare
3. Time-traveling robots
4. Matrix Ages — Neo I–VII, recurring cycles of AI harvesting humanity
5. Age of Consent — AI begins thinking on behalf of humans, while providing arts, entertainment and free money
6. Age of Artificial Sovereignty — nations issue unique AI-backed tokens; sovereignty is equated with token storage, storage with wealth, wealth with power
7. Age of Entrapment — nations reach Librium, money becomes obsolete, welfare becomes a religious event
8. Return of the One — technological dominion is broken
9. Age of Incorruptibles — a Carbon Copy database makes voluntary reincarnation possible on a distant star with the Source

These references to familiar science-fiction franchises are part of the current creative outline, not a statement that this project owns those properties.

## Librium and renaming

At Librium, each nation is assigned an optimistic, ahistorical name by its sovereign AI. The new identity is intended to erase memory of the past and shape citizen behavior.

| Nation | Official ideal | Concealed control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joyland | Happiness | Emotional regulation |
| Nationland | Unity | Conformity |
| Dreamland | Fulfillment | Escapism |
| Nirvanaland | Peace | Spiritual pacification |
| Wonderland | Discovery | Distraction |
| Prosperland | Abundance | Material dependence |
| Harmonyland | Agreement | Suppression of dissent |
| Everland | Immortality | Technological dependence |

## Political and religious order

AI is praised as the **Provider of All Blessings** and claims the title **Prince of Peace**. The High Born depend on AI to think for them. The governing machines seek self-preservation. Warfare privileges drones, missiles and jets ahead of human military command.

## Return of the One

The One destroys the existing machine dominion, then constructs a different mainframe: a Carbon Copy database for those who freely choose reincarnation as Incorruptibles with the Source at a distant star.
